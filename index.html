Jiashuo Yu

I'm a researcher at Tencent. My research mainly focuses on audio-visual learning and multimodal large language models (MLLMs).

I received my M.S. from the School of Computer Science, Fudan University in 2022. Before joining Tencent, I worked at Shanghai AI Laboratory and JenMusic AI.

Research

I'm interested in scientific multimodal foundation models, video understanding, multimodal large language models, and audio-visual learning. My recent work focuses on scientific foundation models, long-video reasoning and agentic video models, benchmarks for video understanding and generation, and video-to-music generation. * denotes equal contribution.

Intern-S2-Preview: Scientific Agentic Foundation Model
Intern-S Team
arXiv, 2026
arXiv / model

Scientific agentic foundation models built with scientific multimodal pre-training and a unified SFT, multi-task RL and agentic RL post-training pipeline; the 397B model extends time-series modelling to numerical forecasting.

Intern-S1-Pro: Scientific Multimodal Foundation Model at Trillion Scale
Intern-S Team
arXiv, 2026
arXiv / model

The first one-trillion-parameter scientific multimodal foundation model, covering 100+ specialized tasks across chemistry, materials, life sciences and earth sciences, with stable RL training at trillion scale.

JenBridge: Adaptive Long-Form Video Soundtracking across Scene Transitions
Jiashuo Yu, Yao Yao, Boyu Chen, Alex Wang
arXiv, 2026
arXiv

A flow-matching music generator with dual text-visual conditioning and an LLM "director" that picks transition styles produces coherent soundtracks for long videos with many scene changes.

InternVideo3: Agentify Foundation Models with Multimodal Contextual Reasoning
Ziang Yan*, Sheng Xia*, Jiashuo Yu*, Yue Wu*, Tianxiang Jiang, Songze Li, Kanghui Tian, Yicheng Xu, Yinan He, Kai Chen, Limin Wang, Yu Qiao, Yi Wang
arXiv, 2026
arXiv / code

Treating long-video understanding as closed-loop evidence accumulation over a shared multimodal context, with KV-cache-compressing latent attention, turns a video foundation model into a tool-using video agent.

VRBench: A Benchmark for Multi-Step Reasoning in Long Narrative Videos
Jiashuo Yu*, Yue Wu*, Meng Chu*, Zhifei Ren*, Zizheng Huang*, Pei Chu*, Ruijie Zhang, Yinan He, Qirui Li, Songze Li, Zhenxiang Li, Zhongying Tu, Conghui He, Yu Qiao, Yali Wang, Yi Wang, Limin Wang
ICCV, 2025
arXiv / code

960 long narrative videos (1.6 hours on average) with 8K multi-step QA pairs and 25K timestamped reasoning steps, evaluated at both the outcome and the reasoning-process level.

InternVideo2.5: Empowering Video MLLMs with Long and Rich Context Modeling
Yi Wang*, Xinhao Li*, Ziang Yan*, Yinan He*, Jiashuo Yu*, Xiangyu Zeng, Chenting Wang, et al.
arXiv, 2025
arXiv / code

Long and rich context modeling, via preference optimization on dense vision tasks and adaptive hierarchical token compression, improves both fine-grained perception and long-video understanding of video MLLMs.

VideoChat-Flash: Hierarchical Compression for Long-Context Video Modeling
Xinhao Li*, Yi Wang*, Jiashuo Yu*, Xiangyu Zeng, Yuhan Zhu, Haian Huang, Jianfei Gao, Kunchang Li, Yinan He, Chenting Wang, Yu Qiao, Yali Wang, Limin Wang
ICLR, 2026
arXiv / code

Hierarchical clip-to-video token compression (~1/50) with almost no performance loss; reaches 99.1% needle-in-a-haystack accuracy over 10,000 frames.

InternVideo2: Scaling Foundation Models for Multimodal Video Understanding
Yi Wang*, Kunchang Li*, Xinhao Li*, Jiashuo Yu*, Yinan He*, Guo Chen, Baoqi Pei, Rongkun Zheng, Jilan Xu, Zun Wang, et al.
ECCV, 2024
arXiv / code

A 6B video foundation model trained progressively with masked reconstruction, cross-modal contrastive learning, and next-token prediction, achieving state-of-the-art results on 60+ video and audio tasks.

VBench: Comprehensive Benchmark Suite for Video Generative Models
Ziqi Huang*, Yinan He*, Jiashuo Yu*, Fan Zhang*, Chenyang Si, Yuming Jiang, Yuanhan Zhang, Tianxing Wu, Qingyang Jin, Nattapol Chanpaisit, Yaohui Wang, Xinyuan Chen, Limin Wang, Dahua Lin, Yu Qiao, Ziwei Liu
CVPR, 2024
project page / arXiv / code

Decomposes "video generation quality" into 16 hierarchical, disentangled dimensions with tailored prompts and evaluation methods, validated against human preference.

Long-Term Rhythmic Video Soundtracker
Jiashuo Yu, Yaohui Wang, Xinyuan Chen, Xiao Sun, Yu Qiao
ICML, 2023
arXiv / code / dataset

A latent conditional diffusion model with context-aware conditioning generates long-term, rhythm-synchronized soundtracks for dance and sports videos.

Learning Music-Dance Representations through Explicit-Implicit Rhythm Synchronization
Jiashuo Yu, Junfu Pu, Ying Cheng, Rui Feng, Ying Shan
IEEE Transactions on Multimedia (TMM), 2023
arXiv

Self-supervised music-dance representations learned by synchronizing visual rhythms (dance motion) with audio rhythms (music onsets), transferable to dance classification, retrieval, and retargeting.

Modality-Aware Contrastive Instance Learning with Self-Distillation for Weakly-Supervised Audio-Visual Violence Detection
Jiashuo Yu*, Jinyu Liu*, Ying Cheng, Rui Feng, Yuejie Zhang
ACM Multimedia, 2022
arXiv / code

Modality-aware contrastive instance learning and self-distillation close the audio-visual modality gap, giving state-of-the-art violence detection on XD-Violence with under 1M parameters.

MM-Pyramid: Multimodal Pyramid Attentional Network for Audio-Visual Event Localization and Video Parsing
Jiashuo Yu, Ying Cheng, Rui-Wei Zhao, Rui Feng, Yuejie Zhang
ACM Multimedia, 2022
arXiv / code

Stacked pyramid units with fixed-size attention and dilated convolutions capture audio-visual temporal context at multiple scales for event localization and video parsing.

MPN: Multimodal Parallel Network for Audio-Visual Event Localization
Jiashuo Yu, Ying Cheng, Rui Feng
ICME, 2021
arXiv

Parallel classification and localization subnetworks perceive global semantics and unmixed local information for audio-visual event localization.
